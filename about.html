About Us

Your Home Away from Home

Marina Guesthouse

A haven for comfort and affordability.

Welcome to Marina Guesthouse, your haven for comfort and affordability in the heart of Windhoek, Namibia. Nestled in the charming Rocky Crest neighborhood, we offer a tranquil escape ideal for both business and leisure travelers. Whether you're here for a short stopover or an extended stay, our warm hospitality and well-equipped rooms ensure a relaxing and enjoyable experience.

More than just a place to stay

At Marina Guesthouse, we believe in providing more than just comfortable accommodation. We strive to create a welcoming atmosphere that feels like a home away from home. Our dedicated staff is available 24/7 to cater to your needs and ensure you have everything for a pleasant stay.

Your gateway to Windhoek

Marina Guesthouse boasts a prime location in Rocky Crest, placing you within easy reach of the city's vibrant attractions. Explore the nearby restaurants, shopping centers, and the central business district, all just a short distance away.
